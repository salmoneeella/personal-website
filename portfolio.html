Project 1: Hack4Philly

Recently, I started an organization called "Hack4Philly," to bring hackathons and resources for product management, UI/UX design, and coding to high school students in Philadelphia.

Our organization is a network of clubs banded together across the city of Philadelphia, and it has been established in 5 different schools for the 2026-2027 school year.

We're also working with Hack Club so that we can create project based learning, and earn rewards for all work done.

Project 2: Gigabites

For this project, we first learned how to program a robot to become a line follower, manually calibrating it with PID tuning. We were then challenged to use our line follower to perform a task, and our team created Gigabites, which is a XRP robot programmed to work as a waiter, providing automated service for customers ordering in cafes.

Our idea was using an XRP robot to simulate a waiter in a restaurant. When a customer places an order, the robot will take note of this, pick up the food, and deliver it to the table.

As the team's coder, the robot took around 4 days of nonstop coding, along with many attempts of trial and error until the robot finally worked. A view of the robot in action is available on Youtube
